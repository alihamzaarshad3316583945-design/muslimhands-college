College programs

HUMANITIES & ARTS

FA

A humanities-focused intermediate program. Students choose elective subjects from the combinations offered by the college and permitted by the relevant board.

Subjects: Electives vary; ask the college for the current subject list.

Typical duration: Two years for intermediate study; confirm the current academic plan.

INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

FA-IT

An arts-focused intermediate program with an information technology focus.

Subjects: Ask the college about current IT subjects and board-approved combinations.

Typical duration: Two years for intermediate study; confirm the current academic plan.

COMPUTER SCIENCE

ICS

Intermediate study combining computer science with mathematics and supporting subjects.

Core focus: Computer Science and Mathematics. Third-subject options can include Physics, Statistics or Economics, subject to board and college availability.

Typical duration: Two years for intermediate study; confirm the current academic plan.

LIFE SCIENCES

FSc Pre-Medical

Science preparation for students interested in biological and health-related fields.

Core subjects: Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

Typical duration: Two years for intermediate study; confirm the current academic plan.

ENGINEERING & PHYSICAL SCIENCES

FSc Pre-Engineering

Science preparation for students interested in engineering and other mathematics-based fields.

Core subjects: Mathematics, Chemistry and Physics.

Typical duration: Two years for intermediate study; confirm the current academic plan.

For all intermediate programs, exact subject combinations, eligibility/merit, board affiliation, fees, class timings and intake dates must be confirmed with the college. No affiliation, fee or schedule details have been assumed here.
